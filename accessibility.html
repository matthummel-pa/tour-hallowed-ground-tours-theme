Accessibility

Access on the field and on this site

We want guests with different mobility, hearing, and digital-access needs to plan a Gettysburg visit with clear information — and to reach us if something is missing.

On-tour access

The Pickett's Charge Deluxe Bus Tour is ADA-accessible and requires no walking beyond short, staff-assisted stops. Walking tours, including Little Round Top, use uneven, sometimes rocky National Park ground and are not wheelchair accessible.

Service animals are welcome. We do not provide wheelchairs. Call (717) 555-0271 at least 48 hours ahead if you need seating near the guide, a slower pace, or help choosing a tour.

Ticket office

271 Baltimore Street has a street-level entrance. The 20-minute loading zone is directly in front. Evening lantern walks meet at the Lincoln Square flagpole on level downtown sidewalks.

This website

This concept aims for WCAG 2.2 AA patterns: skip link, visible focus, labeled form fields, accordion buttons with aria-expanded, and captions on comparison tables. If you hit a barrier, email tours@hallowedground.test with the page URL and what you were trying to do.
